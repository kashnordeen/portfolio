Privacy notice

Keshav Kumar Karn operates this portfolio and project-payment portal. For privacy requests, contact kash.nordeen@gmail.com.

What is collected and why

The payment form collects your name, email, project/invoice reference and agreed amount to identify your project and process/reconcile payment. Backend records include payment/provider references, amount and currency, verification state and timestamps, event metadata and operational verification jobs.

A secure first-party session cookie associates payment status with your originating browser. Session storage retains an opaque request identifier to help prevent duplicate checkout initiation, not the typed customer fields. Rate-limit identifiers are hashed. Hosting providers may also process normal network/access metadata.

Payment processing and service providers

PhonePe handles hosted checkout and payment processing under its own terms and privacy policy. This website does not collect card numbers, UPI PINs or OTPs in its own form. Do not send these details to support.

Supabase stores backend payment records; Vercel hosts the site/backend; QStash delivers opaque verification-job identifiers. The portfolio’s separate contact form sends your name, email and message to Web3Forms, as disclosed on that form; direct email is an alternative. Support email is handled by Gmail. Automated Gmail owner-alert integration is not active.

Retention

Records are retained for the associated project, payment verification and reconciliation, support, refunds, disputes, and applicable accounting or legal recordkeeping. Unresolved payments, disputes or legal obligations may require continued retention. Retention decisions consider the purpose, project status, outstanding issues and applicable obligations; this notice does not assert a universal statutory period.

We do not currently run automatic deletion of payment records. Access is restricted, and requests for deletion or restriction are reviewed against those purposes and obligations. No immediate or automatic-erasure guarantee is made.

Your requests

Email support to request access, correction or deletion of your information, or to raise a privacy concern. Identify the project/payment reference so the relevant records can be located. Verification will be proportionate; do not send passwords, card details, UPI PINs, OTPs or unrelated identity/financial documents. Where continued retention is necessary, we will explain the reason and available restriction options.

Security and limits

Controls include encrypted transport, restricted database access, session-owned status lookup, authenticated callbacks and independent provider-status verification. No system can guarantee absolute security. Project delivery, cancellations and refunds are covered by the linked service policies.
