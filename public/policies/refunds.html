Refunds & cancellations

This policy covers custom project and software payments to Keshav Kumar Karn, individual freelancer. For requests, email kash.nordeen@gmail.com.

Before work starts

If you cancel before agreed work begins, your project payment will be refunded in full, without deducting gateway or administration fees. Payment alone does not mean work has started: kickoff is confirmed in writing and the agreed work must have begun. Any work or purchase requested before kickoff must be specifically agreed and recorded. Terms cannot be added retrospectively.

After work starts

I will provide an itemised account of agreed work completed up to cancellation and refund the unused part of your payment, using prices or rates agreed in writing. There is no additional cancellation penalty or undisclosed hourly rate.

Only specifically authorised, documented and genuinely unrecoverable third-party costs may be considered, subject to your agreement and applicable law. There is no blanket deduction for gateway or third-party costs. Disputed work or costs will be reviewed before being treated as earned. A refund cannot be negative; any separate balance remains subject to your project agreement.

Delivered work and delivery problems

A change of preference alone does not automatically undo payment for properly completed agreed work. Incomplete, deficient, misdescribed or undelivered work will be reviewed against our agreement and your applicable rights. Remedies may include correction, completion or an appropriate refund. Requests remain eligible for review after delivery.

If I cannot continue the project, I will explain why, account for properly completed agreed work and refund unearned payments as appropriate. Delivery and licensing of paid work products remain subject to the project agreement.

Duplicate, failed or pending payments

Send both references if two successful payments cover the same agreed amount. I will verify the excess and check existing refunds, reversals or disputes before repayment to avoid a duplicate refund.

A bank debit, redirect or screenshot alone does not confirm completion or a refund. Do not pay again while a transaction is pending or under review. A failed-payment reversal is different from a merchant refund. I will help reconcile discrepancies through the provider; you may also contact your bank or provider.

Request a refund

Email your project/invoice reference, payment reference, payment date and amount, and reason for the request, using your project email where possible. Additional verification will be proportionate. Never send full card details, passwords, UPI PINs or OTPs, unrelated bank-statement entries or identity documents unless a necessary secure verification process has been explained.

Response and processing targets

  • Acknowledge a request within 48 hours of receipt.
  • Normally explain eligibility and the itemised calculation within 5 business days of receipt.
  • Initiate an approved refund within 5 business days of approval, or sooner where required.
  • Bank/provider credit timing varies; there is no guaranteed credit date.

Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding applicable Indian public holidays. These targets do not delay a mandatory remedy or extend an applicable deadline. If information is missing or an investigation takes longer, I will explain why and give a date for the next update.

Where supported, refunds use the original provider and payment source. I will share the refund reference/status and follow up pending or failed refunds. Initiation is not confirmation of credit. No additional fee will be requested to release a refund.

Your rights

Nothing here excludes rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded or prevents a bank/provider complaint. Mandatory requirements take precedence. Community registration is not covered and remains closed.
