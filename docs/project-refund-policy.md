## 1. Your project agreement

This policy covers custom project and software payments to **[confirmed legal seller]**.
Your scope, price, deliverables, milestones and delivery schedule are agreed in
writing before work begins. An advance or instalment does not by itself settle
your remaining balance or include features outside that agreement.

Work starts when the agreed kickoff is confirmed in writing and the agreed work
has actually begun. Payment alone does not mean work has started. Any work or
purchase requested before kickoff must be specifically agreed and recorded.

## 2. Cancelling before work starts

If you cancel before agreed work begins, we will refund your project payment in
full, without deducting gateway or administration fees. Different terms cannot be
added retrospectively.

## 3. Cancelling after work starts

We will provide an itemised account of agreed work completed up to cancellation
and refund the unused portion of your payment. Completed work is valued at the
price or rates agreed in writing, without an additional cancellation penalty or
an undisclosed hourly rate.

Only specifically authorised, documented and genuinely unrecoverable third-party
costs may be considered, subject to your agreement and applicable law. There is no
blanket deduction for payment-processing or third-party costs. Disputed work and
costs are reviewed, not automatically treated as earned. A refund cannot be
negative; any separate balance is considered under your project agreement.

## 4. Delivered work and delivery problems

A change of preference alone does not automatically undo payment for work properly
completed under the agreed scope. However, incomplete, deficient, misdescribed or
undelivered work will be assessed against the agreement and applicable customer
rights. The remedy may include correction, completion or an appropriate refund.
This is not a blanket refusal of refunds after delivery.

If we cannot continue your project, we will explain why, account for properly
completed agreed work and refund unearned payments as appropriate. Paid work
products remain subject to the agreed delivery and licensing terms.

## 5. Duplicate, failed or pending payments

If two successful payments cover the same agreed amount, contact us with both
references. We will verify and refund the confirmed excess, checking for existing
refunds, reversals or disputes to avoid duplicate repayment.

A bank debit, checkout redirect or screenshot alone does not confirm a completed
payment or refund. If a payment is pending or under review, please do not pay again
until its status has been checked. A failed-payment reversal differs from a
merchant-initiated refund. We will help reconcile discrepancies through the
provider; you can also contact your bank or payment provider.

## 6. Requesting a cancellation or refund

Contact **[confirmed support email; proposed: kash.nordeen@gmail.com]** with your
project/invoice reference, payment reference, payment date and amount, and reason
for the request. Use the email associated with your project where possible.

Any additional verification will be proportionate. Never send full card details,
banking passwords, UPI PINs or OTPs. Do not send unrelated bank-statement
transactions or identity documents unless a necessary, secure verification
process has been explained.

## 7. Response and refund processing

| Stage | Service target |
| --- | --- |
| Acknowledge your request | Within 48 hours of receipt |
| Explain the eligibility decision and itemised calculation | Normally within 5 business days of receipt |
| Initiate an approved refund | Within 5 business days of approval, or sooner where required |
| Credit to your account | Depends on the payment provider, payment method and bank; no guaranteed credit date |

Business days: **[confirm support calendar; proposed Monday–Friday, excluding
applicable Indian public holidays]**. These targets do not delay a mandatory remedy
or extend an applicable deadline. If information is missing or an investigation
needs longer, we will promptly explain why and give a next update date.

Where supported, refunds use the original payment provider and original payment
source. We will share the refund reference and status after initiation and follow
up pending or failed refunds. Initiation is not confirmation of account credit.
We will never request an additional fee to release your refund.

## 8. Your rights and community registration

Nothing here excludes rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded or
prevents a bank/provider complaint. Mandatory requirements take precedence.

This policy does not cover community registration. Community payments remain
closed until the benefits, access/delivery and separate refund terms are defined
and approved.
