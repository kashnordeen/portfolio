## 1. Your project agreement

I am Keshav Kumar Karn, an individual freelancer. This policy covers payments for
my custom project and software services. Before any project payment, we agree by
email on the scope, price, deliverables, milestones, delivery dates and acceptance
criteria. An advance or instalment does not settle any remaining balance or include
extra features.

Work starts once we have confirmed kickoff in writing and I have begun the agreed
work. Payment alone does not mean work has started. Any work or purchase requested
before kickoff must be specifically agreed and recorded.

## 2. Cancelling before work starts

If you cancel before agreed work begins, I will refund your project payment in
full, without deducting gateway or administration fees. Different terms cannot be
added retrospectively.

## 3. Cancelling after work starts

If you cancel after work starts, I will give you an itemised account of the agreed
work completed up to cancellation and refund the unused part of your payment.
I will use the price or rates agreed in writing, with no additional cancellation
penalty or undisclosed hourly rate.

Only specifically authorised, documented and genuinely unrecoverable third-party
costs may be considered, subject to your agreement and applicable law. There is no
blanket deduction for payment-processing or third-party costs. I will review any
disputed work or costs before treating them as earned. A refund cannot be negative;
any separate balance is considered under your project agreement.

## 4. Delivered work and delivery problems

A change of preference alone does not automatically undo payment for work properly
completed under the agreed scope. If work is incomplete, deficient, misdescribed
or undelivered, I will review it against our agreement and your applicable rights.
The remedy may include correction, completion or an appropriate refund. Refund
requests remain eligible for review after delivery.

If I cannot continue your project, I will explain why, account for properly
completed agreed work and refund unearned payments as appropriate. Paid work
products remain subject to the agreed delivery and licensing terms.

## 5. Duplicate, failed or pending payments

If two successful payments cover the same agreed amount, contact me with both
references. I will verify the transactions and refund the confirmed excess after
checking for existing refunds, reversals or disputes to avoid duplicate repayment.

A bank debit, checkout redirect or screenshot alone does not confirm a completed
payment or refund. If a payment is pending or under review, please do not pay again
until its status has been checked. A failed-payment reversal differs from a
merchant-initiated refund. I will help reconcile discrepancies through the
provider; you can also contact your bank or payment provider.

## 6. Requesting a cancellation or refund

Email kash.nordeen@gmail.com with your project/invoice reference, payment reference,
payment date and amount, and the reason for your request. Use your project email
address where possible.

Any additional verification will be proportionate. Never send full card details,
banking passwords, UPI PINs or OTPs. Do not send unrelated bank-statement
transactions or identity documents unless a necessary, secure verification
process has been explained.

## 7. Response and refund processing

| Stage | Service target |
| --- | --- |
| Acknowledge your request | Within 48 hours of receipt |
| Explain the eligibility decision and itemised calculation | Normally within 5 business days of receipt |
| Initiate an approved refund | Within 5 business days of approval, or sooner where required |
| Credit to your account | Depends on the payment provider, payment method and bank; no guaranteed credit date |

Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding applicable Indian public holidays.
These targets do not delay a mandatory remedy or extend an applicable deadline.
If information is missing or an investigation needs longer, I will promptly explain
why and give you a date for the next update.

Where supported, refunds use the original payment provider and original payment
source. I will share the refund reference and status after initiation and follow
up pending or failed refunds. Initiation is not confirmation of account credit.
I will never request an additional fee to release your refund.

## 8. Your rights and community registration

Nothing here excludes rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded or
prevents a bank/provider complaint. Mandatory requirements take precedence.

This policy does not cover community registration. Community payments remain
closed until the benefits, access/delivery and separate refund terms are defined
and approved.
