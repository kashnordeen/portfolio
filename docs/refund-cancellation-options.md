## Option A: original proposal — superseded by the prepared policy

### 1. Scope and payment

This policy covers custom project/software payments to **[confirmed legal seller]**.
The project scope, price, deliverables, milestones and delivery schedule are agreed
in writing before work begins. A payment may be an advance or instalment; it does
not by itself settle a remaining balance or buy features outside that agreement.

For this policy, work starts when the agreed kickoff has been confirmed in writing
and the agreed project work has actually begun. A payment alone does not establish
that work has started. Any work or purchase requested before the normal kickoff
must be specifically agreed and recorded.

### 2. Cancellation before work starts

If you cancel before agreed project work has begun, we will refund your project
payment in full. The proposed policy does not deduct a gateway or administration
fee from this pre-work refund. Any different arrangement needs review and clear
agreement before payment; it cannot be added retrospectively.

### 3. Cancellation after work starts

If you cancel after work has begun, we will give you an itemised account of the
agreed work completed up to cancellation. We will refund the unused portion of
your payment after accounting for that work at the price/rates agreed in writing.
No additional cancellation penalty or undisclosed hourly rate will be imposed.

Only specifically authorised, documented and genuinely unrecoverable third-party
costs may be considered, subject to the agreement and applicable law. A general
statement that all third-party or payment-processing costs are non-refundable is
not part of this draft. Disputed work/costs will be reviewed, not automatically
treated as earned. Refund accounting cannot produce a negative refund; any claim
for a separate balance must be considered under the project agreement.

### 4. Completed work, defects and delivery problems

A change of preference alone does not automatically undo payment for work properly
completed under the agreed scope. However, complaints about incomplete, deficient,
misdescribed or undelivered work will be assessed against that agreement and
applicable customer rights. The proposed remedy may include correction, delivery
of the outstanding work, or an appropriate refund. This clause is not a blanket
refusal of refunds after delivery.

If we cannot continue the project, we will explain the position, account for any
properly completed agreed work, and refund unearned payments as appropriate. Work
products already paid for will be handled according to the agreed delivery and
licensing terms; this draft does not invent an intellectual-property transfer.

### 5. Duplicate, failed or pending payments

If two successful payments cover the same agreed amount, contact us with both
references. We will verify the transactions and refund the confirmed excess, while
checking for any existing refund, reversal or dispute to avoid duplicate repayment.

A bank debit, checkout redirect or screenshot alone does not establish a completed
payment or refund. If the payment is pending or under review, please do not pay
again until its status has been checked. A failed-payment reversal is not the same
as a merchant-initiated refund. We will help reconcile a discrepancy through the
provider; this does not limit your right to contact your bank/payment provider.

### 6. How to request cancellation or a refund

Email **kash.nordeen@gmail.com** (proposed support address; owner to confirm) with
your project/invoice reference, payment reference, payment date/amount and reason.
Use the email associated with the project where possible. Additional verification
will be proportionate; do not send full card details, banking passwords, UPI PINs
or OTPs. Do not send unrelated bank-statement transactions or identity documents
unless a necessary, secure verification process has been explained.

For these proposed business-day targets, Monday–Friday excluding applicable Indian

are not reasons to delay a mandatory remedy or extend any applicable deadline.
If an investigation needs longer, explain the reason and next update date rather
than leaving the request open indefinitely.

Where supported, refunds are processed through the original payment provider to
the original payment source. We will communicate the refund reference and status
after initiation. "Initiated" is not "credited"; pending or failed refunds must
be followed up. We will not request an additional fee to release a refund.

### 8. Rights and community registration

Nothing in this policy excludes rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be
excluded, or prevents a customer from raising a provider/bank complaint. Any
mandatory requirement takes precedence over this proposed commercial policy.

Community registration is not covered by this project policy and is not open for
payment. Its benefits, access/delivery and separate refund rules must be published
and approved before registration checkout is enabled.

## Option B: milestone-funded alternative

Use this instead of Option A's general post-start accounting if projects will
actually be sold in separately priced milestones. Keep Sections 4–8 above.

Before the first payment, record each milestone's price, deliverables, acceptance
criteria and expected delivery date. Request payment for the current milestone
rather than collecting the whole project price by default. A future milestone
that has not begun is fully refundable if prepaid. If the current milestone is
cancelled midway, account for agreed work actually completed, refund its unused
funds and provide an itemised explanation. Payment or silence alone does not
automatically make a milestone accepted or the payment fully earned.

Properly delivered milestones are assessed against the agreement, with defect,
non-delivery and other applicable remedies preserved. No fixed deposit forfeiture,
automatic acceptance deadline or non-refundable percentage is invented here.
